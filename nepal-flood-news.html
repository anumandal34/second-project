Bhadra 10 Bhote Koshi flood: heavy loss of life and property

Flood date: Wednesday, 10 Bhadra 2083 (26 August 2026), 8:40 AM  |  Source: Ministry of Home Affairs

A massive flood in the Bhote Koshi River of Rasuwa District caused heavy loss of life and property. Riverbank areas in Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading and Gorkha were severely affected. The government started search, rescue and emergency relief operations, and damage assessment was reported to be ongoing.
